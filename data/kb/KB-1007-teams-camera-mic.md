title: Teams camera or microphone not working
category: Applications
keywords: teams, camera, webcam, microphone, mic, audio, video, not detected, meeting
---
## Symptoms
Teams says no camera or microphone was found, or others cannot see or hear you.

## Steps
1. Close other apps that may be using the camera (Zoom, browser tabs with video).
2. In Windows Settings > Privacy & security, allow Camera and Microphone access for apps.
3. In Teams, open Settings > Devices and pick the right camera, microphone and speaker.
4. Unplug and plug back in any USB headset or webcam, then restart Teams.
5. Check the laptop has no physical camera shutter or mute key turned on.

## Raise a ticket if
The device does not appear in Windows Device Manager at all.
