## Symptoms
You cannot sign in because you forgot your password, it expired, or your account is locked after too many attempts.

## Steps
1. DeskVoice can start a self-service password reset or account unlock for you. Just say "reset my password" or "unlock my account".
2. You will get a reset link on your registered phone or personal email.
3. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters that you have not used before.
4. Sign in again on all devices (laptop, phone mail app, Teams) with the new password.

## Raise a ticket if
You do not receive the reset link, or your account locks again straight after a reset.
