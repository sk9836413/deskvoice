title: Printer queue stuck or not printing
category: End-user Devices
keywords: printer, print, queue, stuck, not printing, offline, paper
---
## Symptoms
Documents stay in the print queue, or the printer shows as offline.

## Steps
1. Check the printer is on, has paper and shows no error on its screen.
2. Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers, select the printer and open the print queue. Cancel all documents.
3. Restart the printer and your computer.
4. Make sure you are on the office network or VPN, then print a test page.
5. If the printer still shows offline, remove it and add it again from the company printer list.

## Raise a ticket if
The printer is offline for everyone, or shows a hardware error.
