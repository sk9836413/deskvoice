## Symptoms
The laptop shows it is connected to Wi-Fi but websites do not load.

## Steps
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, or forget the network and connect again.
2. Check if other devices on the same Wi-Fi have internet.
3. Open Command Prompt and run `ipconfig /release` then `ipconfig /renew`.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. In the office, try a different access point or a wired port.

## Raise a ticket if
Several people in the same area have no internet. That may be a network outage.
