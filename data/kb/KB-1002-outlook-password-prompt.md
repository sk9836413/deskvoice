title: Outlook keeps asking for the password
category: Messaging
keywords: outlook, password prompt, credentials, keeps asking, sign in again, login popup
---
## Symptoms
Outlook shows a password or sign-in window again and again, even after you type the right password.

## Steps
1. Check that your password has not expired by signing in at the company web portal.
2. Close Outlook. Open Credential Manager > Windows Credentials and remove entries that mention "MicrosoftOffice" or "Outlook".
3. Open Outlook again and sign in once with your work email and current password.
4. If you changed your password recently, lock and unlock your computer so Windows picks up the new password.

## Raise a ticket if
The prompt keeps coming back after clearing saved credentials.
