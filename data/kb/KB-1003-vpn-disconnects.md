## Symptoms
The VPN connects but drops every few minutes, or will not stay connected on home Wi-Fi.

## Steps
1. Disconnect the VPN, turn Wi-Fi off and on, and reconnect.
2. If possible, move closer to the router or use a wired connection.
3. Turn off any other VPN or proxy apps that are running.
4. Restart the VPN client completely (exit it from the system tray, then open it again).
5. Make sure the VPN client is up to date from the company software portal.

## Raise a ticket if
The VPN still drops on a stable connection, or you see a certificate or authentication error.
