## Symptoms
You see "Access denied" or "You need permission" when opening a shared folder, drive or SharePoint site.

## Steps
1. Click "Request access" on the page if it is shown. The site owner gets an email.
2. Or ask DeskVoice: "I need access to the finance drive". It will raise an access request to your manager for approval.
3. Access is usually added within one working day of approval. Sign out and back in to refresh it.

## Raise a ticket if
Access was approved but you still cannot open the location after signing out and in.
