---
id: KB-1005
title: Set up MFA on a new phone
category: Identity & Access
keywords: mfa, authenticator, new phone, two factor, 2fa, verification code, lost phone
---
## Symptoms
You changed or lost your phone and can no longer approve sign-in requests.

## Steps
1. If you still have the old phone, sign in to the security info page and add the new phone first.
2. Install the authenticator app on the new phone and scan the QR code from the security info page.
3. Remove the old phone from the list once the new one works.
4. If you no longer have the old phone, IT must verify your identity and reset your MFA.

## Raise a ticket if
You lost the old phone or cannot sign in at all. IT will verify your identity before resetting MFA.
